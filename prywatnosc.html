Ciasteczka i pamięć przeglądarki

Ściana boksów nie używa ciasteczek, reklam ani narzędzi do śledzenia czy statystyk odwiedzin. W pamięci przeglądarki (localStorage, IndexedDB) zapisuje tylko to, bez czego strona nie działa:

  • logowanie do zespołu (Google Firebase),
  • Twoje ustawienia: język, otwarta zakładka, wybrane imię, obrót mapy, odsłuch radia, zamknięte komunikaty.

Możesz to w każdej chwili usunąć, czyszcząc dane tej strony w przeglądarce. Strona i jej biblioteki są pobierane z serwerów GitHub Pages, Google (gstatic.com) i Cloudflare (cdnjs) – jak przy każdej stronie, te serwery widzą Twój adres IP.

Who is responsible

Maciej Szczepaniak (FR Mods). Contact: or the “Report a problem” form in the app or on the pit wall.

Cookies and browser storage

The pit wall does not use cookies, ads, tracking or visitor statistics. It stores in your browser (localStorage, IndexedDB) only what the page needs to work:

  • your team login (Google Firebase),
  • your settings: language, open tab, chosen name, map rotation, radio listening, dismissed notices.

You can delete this at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser. The page and its libraries are loaded from GitHub Pages, Google (gstatic.com) and Cloudflare (cdnjs) – as with any website, those servers see your IP address.

Crash reports (with consent)

On first start the program asks whether to send crash reports to the author. A report contains the error description, program version, language and the log of the last session (the engineer's messages and what it recognised from your speech). Before sending, keys, passwords, e-mail addresses and your Windows user name are removed. When you report a problem yourself, you decide whether to attach the log. You can change your consent at any time in the app: Help → Report a problem.

Reports and crash reports – up to 12 months, or less once the issue is resolved. Team data – as long as the team uses the program; the team can delete it, and the author deletes it on request.

Legal basis and your rights

Team data is processed to provide the service you use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); crash reports and logs – based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)); problem reports – in the legitimate interest of fixing the program (Art. 6(1)(f)). You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict processing, to object, to data portability and to withdraw consent at any time. You can also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country.
